DIPLOMA PROGRAMS

4-Year Diploma in Engineering Technologies

BTEB-approved curriculum offering 8 comprehensive semesters of theoretical, laboratory, and industrial attachment training.

Computer Technology

Duration: 4 Years · 8 Semesters

Focuses on software engineering, web technologies, computer networking, AI integration, hardware maintenance, and cyber security fundamentals.

  • High-speed Network & Server Lab
  • Python, Java, JavaScript & Database
  • IT Industry Placement Assistance

Civil Technology

Duration: 4 Years · 8 Semesters

Covers modern structural engineering, surveying instruments, soil mechanics, concrete technology, and AutoCAD/BIM design for infrastructure development.

  • Advanced Surveying & Total Station Lab
  • Material Testing & Hydraulics Lab
  • Real-world Site Inspection Training

Electrical Technology

Duration: 4 Years · 8 Semesters

Specializes in power transmission, industrial circuit wiring, substation automation, renewable energy, and electrical machinery controls.

  • High-Voltage Power & Machine Lab
  • Industrial Automation & PLC Training
  • Direct Substation Field Attachments

Mechanical Technology

Duration: 4 Years · 8 Semesters

Instruction in thermodynamics, automobile engineering, machine design, CNC lathe machining, manufacturing processes, and industrial safety.

  • Dedicated Lathe, Welding & CNC Workshops
  • Automobile Engine Testing Setup
  • Heavy Machinery Maintenance

Textile Technology

Duration: 4 Years · 8 Semesters

Trains students for Bangladesh's booming garments sector: yarn manufacturing, fabric design, dyeing, wet processing, and garment quality control.

  • Spinning, Weaving & Wet Processing Lab
  • Garments Merchandising Curriculum
  • 100% Industry Demand in RMG
